Three things I decided while building it — say if any is wrong

  • Fixed: the second baby. The falling bundle used animation-fill-mode: both with a 2.6s delay, and both tells the browser to paint frame 0 during the delay — so a motionless baby hung in the sky from page load until the stork reached it. It is forwards now, which leaves the element hidden until its moment. One baby, carried in and dropped.
  • The paperwork now disappears completely before the wordmark arrives, over a full second, drifting up slightly as it fades. The sky is empty for an instant — that pause is what makes the wordmark land.
  • The wordmark thickens rather than just appearing. It comes up from 16px below at weight 300 with open letter-spacing, and lands at 700 with normal spacing. That is what "bolden and come up" reads as in motion.
  • Next arrives last and stays put forever. It never moves after it lands, so a thumb already heading for it is never chasing a moving target.
The one real problem, and it is not cosmetic. The button appears at 8.5 seconds. Your own load bar is 2s and M1 is a personalised view in under five minutes — eight and a half seconds of animation before a parent can act is a long time, and on the second visit it is simply an obstacle. So I built in two escapes: a tap anywhere skips straight to the end state (try "Skip to end"), and in the real build the whole sequence should be remembered — seen once, never again; returning visitors land on the held final frame. If you would rather not rely on that, the other lever is to compress: the same six beats fit in about 4.5s without feeling rushed.
