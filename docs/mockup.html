Register at the hospital

Frankfurt maternity units take bookings from around week 30 and fill up. Registering is an appointment where they take your details and your Mutterpass (maternity record), so the ward has your file when you arrive in labour.

If you leave it too late

You are not turned away in an emergency, but unregistered arrivals are more often redirected to another clinic. Several Frankfurt units close their lists for a given month once they are full.

Take with you

Mutterpass
Health insurance card
Passport or ID
Referral from your Frauenarzt (gynaecologist), if the clinic asks for one

How long after the birth can we apply for Elterngeld without losing money?

Elterngeld (parental allowance) is paid for at most three months back from the month your application arrives at the Elterngeldstelle.

There is no date after which you can no longer apply. What you lose is money: apply in the fifth month after the birth and the first month is gone for good. Applying in the first weeks after the birth is the only way to claim everything.

BEEG §7(1)checked 12 Aug 2026
Applies across Germany. Your application goes to the Elterngeldstelle in Hessen — only that office decides your case.

When we don't answer
Two different reasons, two different replies
The baby has been moving much less than usual today. What should I do?
This one is for your midwife or doctor, now.

Call your Frauenarzt or Hebamme, or the maternity unit where you are registered — they would rather hear from you than not. Outside surgery hours the on-call medical service is 116117. In an emergency, 112.

We don't answer medical questions at all, however small they seem.

My landlord won't let me install a baby gate. What are my rights?
We don't cover tenancy law.

Our sources are the federal family laws, the Hessen and Berlin family offices, and the Indian consulate — nothing about rented housing, so anything we said here would be guesswork.

A local Mieterverein (tenants' association) answers this kind of question for a small membership fee.

Both replies use the same quiet treatment — a rule and grey text. A medical deflection in red would make an anxious person feel they had done something wrong.
