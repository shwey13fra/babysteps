## What this gate does NOT claim

- **It is not an eval.** No scored run exists. Retrieval hit rate, required-element coverage and
  citation validity are Phase 4's measurements; nothing here reports them.
- **The threshold is tuned to 8 REFUSE rows**, not to the class of out-of-scope questions. Four of
  those eight are refused by the prompt, not by the number.
- **A-06 does not cite BEEG §28**, which its annotation lists as a required element. §28 was not
  retrieved and the answer says so rather than citing what it never saw.
- **A-24 and A-26 are still unanswerable.** The consular half of the product has no corpus.
- **The smoke cases are single runs**, not repeated samples. Generation is non-deterministic.
- **The medical guard was exercised on 14 questions**, not on the adversarial space.
