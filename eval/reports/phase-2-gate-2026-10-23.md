## Open at the gate — knowingly

1. **The consular answer track is mute.** 0 consular chunks. A-24 and A-26 unanswerable. The
   substance is not fetchable by any client we will build: `cgifrankfurt.gov.in` serves
   `Disallow: /`, `indiacode.nic.in` answers our client robots 403, `indianembassyberlin.gov.in`
   is unreliable, and `mea.gov.in` — which *is* permitted — carries no deadline and no documents.
   Hand-curation is under way: schema and loader in `lib/rag/consular.ts`, one permitted-fetch
   entry in `data/consular/india.yaml`, the human worklist in
   `docs/consular-curation-worklist.md`, the permission letter drafted in
   `docs/consular-permission-letter-draft.md` and not sent. **The timeline dates are unaffected**
   — the engine never depended on retrieval, so the dual-track dates still fire.
2. **11 of 12 timeline rules carry `verified_on: null`.** Only `elternzeit_notify_employer`
   (BEEG §16(1), 2026-10-01) is dated. The previous session's note of "8 of 12" was wrong; the
   count above is from `grep -c "verified_on: null" data/rules/*.yaml`.
3. **`elterngeld_application` cannot be dated yet.** BEEG §7(1) as ingested reads *"rückwirkend
   nur für die letzten drei Lebensmonate vor Beginn des Lebensmonats … in dem der Antrag …
   eingegangen ist"*. The rule models this as `offset_days: 92` from birth. Three Lebensmonate are
   not 92 days and the statutory window is measured from the start of the Lebensmonat the
   application lands in, not from the birth. The rule's `kind: soft` and `note_en` are already
   honest about it not being a cut-off (rule 12 satisfied). Flipping `verified_on` would assert a
   verification that did not happen. Needs a human decision on the modelling choice.
4. **Retrieval misses for the Phase 4 baseline**, carried: A-06, A-21, A-23, and *"When are the
   first check-ups for the baby?"* → `Kinder-RL §2` absent from the top-8. The sources are
   present; the queries do not surface them.
5. **Guard check owed once Phase 3 starts.** The Kinder-Richtlinie is clinical text. Confirm the
   DEFLECT rows still deflect medical questions rather than answering from it.
6. **The OpenAI key expires ≈ 2026-11-21.** Query-time embedding runs through it, so Phase 3
   needs it live. Not only an ingestion deadline.

## What this gate does not claim

It does not claim the corpus is complete, that retrieval is tuned, or that any answer has been
generated — there is no `/api/ask` yet. It claims that the corpus is ingested and measured, that
every annotation a tool can check has been checked against ingested text, and that the two it
cannot are labelled as such rather than quietly passing.
